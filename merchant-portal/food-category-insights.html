2 · Recommended, not yet built

Proposed earlier for a specific, measurable effect rather than a generic reach goal.
Exclusive

Peak-Hours Spotlight reservation

Reserve the exclusive lunch (11am–1pm) or dinner (6–9pm) Spotlight on the Food folder — one restaurant per window, per city.

Why: Already built as a feature — this formalizes it as a purchasable parameter alongside everything else a merchant already understands.
Seasonal

Festival-window targeting

Reach customers in the 3-day run-up to a named festival (Diwali, Navratri, Holi) with a festival offer.

Why: Festival spending is concentrated and predictable — captures gifting/entertaining purchases in the exact window they happen.
Retention

Early-decline frequency targeting

Reach loyal customers whose visit cadence has started slipping (was every 10 days, now 20+) — before they've fully lapsed.

Why: The existing "haven't ordered in X days" rule only catches customers already gone. Catching the decline early is cheaper than a full win-back.
Growth

Basket-gap cross-sell

Reach frequent customers who've never ordered from a specific menu category.

Why: Avyaya has itemized data most loyalty tools don't — turns it into a precise upsell lever. See Section 3 — Amma's own data sharpens this considerably.
Revenue smoothing

Slow-day fill targeting

Reach customers to fill a merchant-identified slow day or time slot.

Why: Addresses a concrete, quantifiable problem — easy for a merchant to measure before/after.
Acquisition

New-to-neighborhood acquisition

Reach customers whose very first invoice in the area was logged in the last 30 days.

Why: New movers haven't formed habits yet — the highest-leverage acquisition window that exists.

Recent add-on attach-rate gap

✓ Validated · Live

Reach repeat customers whose drink/dessert attach rate over a recent window sits below their own historical norm — replacing the weaker "never ordered X" lifetime framing.

Window: last 30 days last 60 days last 90 days
Only 0.5% of regulars (5+ visits) have never ordered a dessert — lifetime framing finds almost nobody. Looking at the last 30 days instead: of 388 customers with 2+ visits, 12.4% (48 customers) sit below Amma's own 34% baseline attach rate — a real, reachable segment the old framing missed entirely.

Quick-visit upgrade

✓ Validated · Live

4 · Tested and ruled out

Two plausible-sounding parameters that the data didn't support — shown rather than quietly dropped.
✕ No signal

Order-type-specific dessert/drink targeting

Hypothesis: delivery or takeaway customers skip add-ons more than dine-in. Attach rates were nearly identical across all three — dessert 30.9% / 29.4% / 30.4%, drink 51.0% / 49.5% / 49.3% for Dine-in / Takeaway / Delivery. Not worth a dedicated parameter.

✕ Too narrow

Lifetime "never ordered this category" for regulars

Among customers with 5+ visits, 0–0.5% have never ordered Dessert, Drinks, or Breakfast — almost everyone eventually orders everything. Superseded by the recent-window attach-rate gap above, which finds a real segment the lifetime version can't.

Source: 10,009 Amma invoices (merchant-portal Firestore data, Oct 2026) · analysis script scripts/analyze-amma-categories.mjs
